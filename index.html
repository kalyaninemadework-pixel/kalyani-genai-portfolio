Klyra AI Architecture & Pipeline

Klyra AI acts as an intelligent intermediary between raw human briefs and generative latent diffusion models. Built with a modular Python backend.

STAGE 1: Intent Dissection & Entity Tagging

Extracts subject, mood, camera framing, lighting style, and commercial constraints from client input.

STAGE 2: Latent Weight Expansion & Syntax Translation

Injects verified optical terminology (e.g. 85mm f/1.4, volumetric caustics) and engine parameters (--v 6.0, --stylize, --chaos).

STAGE 3: Negative Prompt & Artifact Purging

Dynamically binds negative seeds to eliminate anatomical anomalies, excessive digital noise, and brand violations.
